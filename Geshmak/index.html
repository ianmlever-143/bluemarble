    How to play

    Three Yids a puzzle: a Today Yid, a Yester Yid and a Really Old Yid. Everyone gets the same three.

    Each one comes with three clues. Name them on the first clue for 54 points, the second for 36, the third for 18. A wrong guess flips the next clue. Take all the time you want.

    Then the bonus: the three are connected. Guess the thread for 18 more. A perfect day is 180, which is ten times chai.

    What's "Geshmak"?

    Geshmak (guh-SHMAHK) is Yiddish for delicious, delightful, a pleasure. It shares a root with the German Geschmack, "taste." You can eat something geshmak, and you can also do something with a geshmak, meaning with real relish.

    For generations the famous Yiddish sigh was "S'iz shver tsu zayn a Yid": it's hard to be a Jew. Sholem Aleichem even made it the title of a play.

    The comeback, a favorite in Hasidic teaching, flips one word: "S'iz geshmak tsu zayn a Yid." It's delicious to be a Jew.

    And Yid? It's simply the Yiddish word for Jew. Here it's said the way it's said around the Shabbat table: with love.

    I love being Jewish and I love making games!

    To build Jewish connection. Play it, share it, argue about it at dinner.
