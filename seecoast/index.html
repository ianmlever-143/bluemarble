      How SeeCoast Works

      A new game every so often. Five spots each. One map.
      • 1Read the quick clue, then tap the map where you think it is.
      • 2We measure how far your pin was from the real spot, in miles.
      • 3Every guess gets stamped with one of four tiers — see below.
      • 4Jump between rounds anytime — a guess locks the moment you confirm it.
      • 5Finish all 5 rounds to see your total miles off. Your overall rank uses your middle score, so one wild miss won't sink you.
